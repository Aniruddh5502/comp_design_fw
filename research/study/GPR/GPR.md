Gaussian Processes doesn't work with a single function fitting the whole training data like Neural Network does make a single function to fit all the total data. Rather it gives us a family of function that all the member of that family satisfies our training data. It's like that problem from middle school. How many lines can pass through a point. The answer is a fammily of lines, it can be a family of circles also, combinedly we can say a family of curves. 

Now consider you have multiple points each one is one of your design points. So when you want to get some function that satisfies all of your training data, you find curves that goes through your training points. Here it's not necessary that there should be only one function like in the case of Neural Network. So there can be a family of curves that satisfies all the training points. This family of curves is what the Gaussian Process gives us.

And this family of functions are exact solutions (ig) for the training data.
