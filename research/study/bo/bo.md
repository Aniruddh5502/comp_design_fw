Bayesian Optimizaition is an Optimization Algorithm and it operates in 2 settings.

1. When its used as an Optimizer for a surrogate model: In that case the BO algorithm takes the uncertainity measurement from the surrogate models and operates on the constraints to find the design point for given constraints.

2. When its used to build the surrogate model: At this setup the algoritm optimizes the hyperparameters of the surrogate model to make it usable.
