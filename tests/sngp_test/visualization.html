Doctoral Analysis: Parametric FEM Surrogates

The Reliability Tax

Exploring the precision-stability trade-off in Spectral Normalized Gaussian Processes (SNGP).

The Core Finding

Our analysis identifies a structural Reliability Tax: the necessary loss in predictive precision accepted to guarantee global stability. As the Lipschitz constant λ is tightened, the surrogate is prevented from overfitting local curvatures, resulting in a measurable increase in RMSE.
