#### ML-leaning, if you emphasize the modeling and validation side

11. Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (Elsevier)

13. Applied Soft Computing (Elsevier)

15. Reliability Engineering & System Safety (Elsevier), a good fit if you include uncertainty quantification


#### Mechanics and structures, if you frame it around a specific component or design

16. Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing (Elsevier)
17. Mechanism and Machine Theory (Elsevier)
18. Engineering Structures (Elsevier)
19. Thin-Walled Structures (Elsevier)
20. Journal of Computational Physics (Elsevier), a stretch unless your method is novel

**Always check in scimagojr.com for Journals Quartile ranking.**

Acceptance odds: Editors at these journals mostly reject **pipeline papers** with **no new contribution**. **A paper that only applies standard surrogate methods to one geometry usually fares worse than one that shows a general method, a benchmark against other surrogates, and experimental or high-fidelity validation**. I’d start with journals 1 to 5 and treat the ML-leaning ones as a second tier.
